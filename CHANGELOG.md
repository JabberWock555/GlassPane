# Changelog

## 0.1.0

First public release.

- **Dashboard for old tablets:** runs on Chrome 57 (2017) and up, with a
  frosted-glass look that costs the tablet nothing.
- **Rooms sidebar** that collapses to an icon rail, plus a **Home** view of
  what's on now (or your most-used devices).
- **Ceiling-fan tiles:** speed slider, power button and the fan's built-in
  light on one tile.
- **Lights, switches and sensors**, and a live-synced **shopping list**
  (any Home Assistant to-do list).
- **Clock with air quality (AQI)** for your home location.
- **Photo screensaver:** photos are resized on the server for each
  screen's exact size.
- **Settings page**, shared by every screen; full screen and keep-awake on
  the first tap.
- **Safe by default:** the tablet never sees your Home Assistant token and
  can only control devices on the dashboard.
- **Runs anywhere:** Docker, a native binary, or an old Android phone with
  Termux.
